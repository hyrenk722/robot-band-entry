Entry for the make.fun competition "Name the Robot Band": The Sentient Vacuums — their hit 'I Suck, Therefore I Am' cleaned up at the awards.
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> The Sentient Vacuums — their hit 'I Suck, Therefore I Am' cleaned up at the awards.

Why this entry: The band name is a real joke, not a pun: sentient vacuums are absurd on their own, and the hit song twists Descartes ("I think, therefore I am") into "I Suck, Therefore I Am", which only a vacuum could sing. The closer "cleaned up at the awards" adds a second laugh that fits the band exactly. It sounds like a band name worth repeating to a friend, and the whole entry is one tight line with no filler and no explanation of the joke.
